# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led.

- Critique prompts, code, tests, and claims, not people.
- Do not post private data, credentials, or unlicensed personal material.
- Clearly distinguish observed behaviour, official documentation, inference, and recommendation.
- Reproduce platform claims with the surface, model, mode, date, and relevant settings.
- Do not use issues or pull requests for harassment, spam, impersonation, or deceptive content.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.
